IMPORTANT - read before relying on this in production:

1. Single writer only. `replicaCount` must stay at 1 - do not attach an HPA
   to this Deployment. Rollouts use `strategy: Recreate`, so there is a brief
   window of downtime on every deploy while the old pod terminates before the
   new one starts. This is intentional: SQLite cannot tolerate two writers.

2. First deploy against an empty bucket: the `litestream-restore` init
   container uses `-if-replica-exists`, so it's a no-op the first time and
   flagrant-api starts with a fresh database. `litestream-replicate` then
   begins seeding {{ .Values.litestream.replica.bucket }} immediately.

3. Shutdown race window: `litestream-replicate` and `flagrant-api` are
   ordinary sidecar containers (for broad Kubernetes-version compatibility),
   so both receive SIGTERM at the same time rather than the sidecar draining
   after the app stops. Up to one Litestream sync interval (default ~1s) of
   writes made in the last instant before shutdown could be lost. If your
   cluster runs Kubernetes 1.29+, consider converting litestream-replicate to
   a native sidecar (`initContainers` entry with `restartPolicy: Always`) for
   stronger ordering guarantees.
